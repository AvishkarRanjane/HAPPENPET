About HappenPet Store

Welcome to HappenPet

HappenPet Store is India's premier destination for luxury pet products and accessories. Founded in 2020, we have been dedicated to providing the finest quality products for your beloved pets. From premium dog beds to exquisite cat towers, we curate only the best for your furry friends.

Our mission is to ensure every pet owner has access to premium, comfortable, and stylish products that enhance the quality of life for their pets. We believe that pets deserve nothing but the best, and that's exactly what we deliver.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide luxury pet products that enhance the comfort, health, and happiness of pets while maintaining the highest standards of quality and customer service.

Vision: To become the most trusted luxury pet store in India, known for excellence in product quality, customer satisfaction, and pet care innovation.
